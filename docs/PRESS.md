## In one line

_Racing team simulation game based in a post-petrol world, where the last petrolheads ply their craft._

The short pitch: _Dwarf Fortress meets MF Ghost meets Aussie car culture._

## About the game

Petrol is the rarest thing in the world, and a handful of people still race combustion cars on it. In 2031 they meet on closed Australian public roads for a new championship, six rounds a season, in ordinary road-legal cars. You run a garage. You buy and upgrade the cars, sign the drivers, keep the money coming in, and try to build a name that lasts through the seasons. Every driver, car, garage and race is remembered.

It is a management game first: as much a garage simulation as a racing one. The people, the parts, the suppliers and the books matter as much as the lap times. The names, the world and the branding are all original, and the idea is loosely inspired by _MF Ghost_.

## What is in 0.2.0

- A world of 96 garages and 313 drivers racing six rounds a season on closed Australian roads, simulated sector by sector, for as many seasons as you like, played on a calendar with a date (Continue, +1 day, +1 week, Next event).
- Buy cars new, used or at auction, fit upgrade parts, and restore a worn chassis.
- Sign and train drivers, hire mechanics and executives, build facilities, and negotiate with sponsors, makers and suppliers in five tiers, with outages and a spot market.
- Every garage has an owner, a cap table, a valuation, dividends and a board, and the computer garages run as businesses of their own.
- A ledger by year with every transaction, daily life between rounds, and a people layer: morale, contacts, social occasions and the press.
- A Registry with a profile for every driver, car, garage, company, prelim and race, and an Almanac of champions, awards and record leaderboards.
- Australian licence plates in each state's own format, and a flag beside every driver and garage.

The full list is in the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md), and the [how to play guide](HOW_TO_PLAY.md) walks through a first season.
